Privacy notice

Updated September 11, 2026.

Current app

Ahead is a personal-use iPhone app under development. Plans and imported calendar information are stored locally on the device. With permission, it reads selected calendars available through Apple Calendar to display upcoming commitments and support preparation. Adding an event uses Apple's calendar editor and requires user approval.

The current implementation does not send calendar contents or plans to a developer-operated server, analytics provider, advertising service, or AI provider. Household cloud sharing is not yet implemented. Device backups may be handled by Apple according to your device settings.

Gmail integration

Gmail integration is being developed and is not currently connected. Configuring an OAuth client does not grant inbox access. Planned access is read-only, authorized separately by each account owner, to identify family-related commitments and preparation details. Ahead will not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Before Gmail processing or cloud sharing is enabled, this notice will be updated to specify actual processing, storage, retention, deletion, and sharing practices. No external AI processing is currently implemented.

Ahead's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data will not be sold or used for advertising.

Your controls

You can revoke calendar permission in iPhone Settings. Revocation stops access but does not erase previously stored local information. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data; device backups are managed separately through Apple. Any future Google authorization can be revoked through Google Account connections.

This website

These public pages contain no household information and use no analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

The developer is yelramy on GitHub. During this private development phase, participants can contact the developer directly. Do not post private calendar or email information in public GitHub issues. Google's consent screen also provides the configured support contact.
