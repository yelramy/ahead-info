Privacy notice

Updated September 11, 2026.

Current app

Ahead is a personal-use iPhone app under development. Plans and imported calendar information are stored locally on the device. With permission, it reads selected calendars available through Apple Calendar to display upcoming commitments and support preparation. Adding an event uses Apple's calendar editor and requires user approval.

Calendar contents and email information are never sent to a developer-operated server, analytics provider, advertising service, or cloud AI provider. Device backups may be handled by Apple according to your device settings.

Household sharing (optional)

You may sign in with Apple and create or join a household so that plans (a plan's title, date, owner, checklist progress, proposal text, and agreed/ready status) sync between household members' iPhones. Only plans are shared. Imported calendar events, emails, email previews, and per-phone reminders are never uploaded and never visible to another member.

Shared plans are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the developer's personal Google Cloud project (US multi-region), protected by Firebase Authentication and security rules that restrict every read and write to the household's listed members. Joining requires an invite code that works once and expires after seven days. The developer does not run analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. Firebase and Google's infrastructure process this data under Google's Firebase privacy terms.

Sign in with Apple provides a stable anonymous identifier and, on first sign-in, your name; Ahead stores the name you choose to show other members. "Stop sharing on this iPhone" disconnects the device; plans already in the household remain there for other members. Deleting a plan removes it for all members.

Gmail integration

The private TestFlight build offers optional Google sign-in with read-only Gmail access. Each account owner authorizes access directly with Google. Ahead does not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

While Ahead is open, it lists up to 300 emails from the last 30 days, excluding spam, trash, chats, and Gmail's Promotions, Social, and Forums categories. Each new message is retrieved directly from Google to your iPhone. Subject, sender, received date, message identifiers, a bounded excerpt of the message text (up to 6,000 characters), attachment names, and text extracted from up to three small attachments (PDF, calendar files, plain text, and photos via on-device text recognition) are stored in the app's local database on your iPhone only. Messages are suggestions, not automatically confirmed commitments. Linking an email to a calendar event requires your confirmation unless the title matches exactly one calendar event.

Google's sign-in SDK manages authorization credentials using the iPhone Keychain. No Gmail information is sent to a developer-operated backend, another household member, or any cloud AI service. No background mailbox monitoring is implemented.

On-device reading (Apple Intelligence)

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Ahead uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to read calendar event titles and email text (including extracted attachment text) and decide whether an item needs preparation, and to pull out a date, time, place, and deadline. This processing happens entirely on your iPhone; nothing is sent to Apple's servers or any third party by Ahead. The results (a yes/no, a short reason, and any extracted date or place) are stored locally next to the event or email. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, Ahead uses only your rules and a fixed keyword list. Rules you create (words or calendars to always or never plan for) are stored on your iPhone and always override the automatic decision.

Saved previews remain on your iPhone until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting an individual preview clears its subject, sender, preview, message text, and attachment text but retains source identifiers to prevent automatic reimport. Disconnecting Gmail revokes authorization but does not erase previously imported information. Device backups are managed separately through Apple. Google's own processing is governed by its policies.

Ahead's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data will not be sold or used for advertising.

Your controls

You can revoke calendar permission in iPhone Settings. Revocation stops access but does not erase previously stored local information. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data; device backups are managed separately through Apple. Google authorization can also be revoked through Google Account connections.

This website

These public pages contain no household information and use no analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.
